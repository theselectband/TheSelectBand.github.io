The Select is a DIY punk band formed in early 2021 from the suburbs of Tigard, Oregon. We play fast, high energy music with a classic punk rock edge. Our live show is an explosion of frenzied guitar riffs held together by thunderous drums and intricate bass lines. We released our debut self recorded and produced 5 song EP "Defective Demonstration" in the spring of 2024. Check out our socials to keep up to date on shows and music!
